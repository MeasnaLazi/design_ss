# Dark five-column phone strip (variable device count per beat)

## Overview

Horizontal **five-column** phone-strip layout on one canvas: each column is a **single marketing beat** with **upper** typographic zone and **lower** device cluster. Suited to apps that combine **multimedia journaling**, **voice capture**, **timeline navigation**, **AI-assisted editing**, and **list-style history management** — described only as **layout roles**, not fixed copy.

## Strip and background

- **Format:** **five** tall phone-aspect columns in a row; one continuous **horizontal** strip (not separate exported files unless the brief says otherwise).
- **Background:** uniform **very dark** field (near black) across all columns; no busy texture behind headline blocks.
- **Separation:** clear **vertical gutters** between columns so each beat reads as its own column while the background stays continuous.
- **Readability:** headline and subtitle use **high contrast** (light on dark).

## Text layers

- **Placement:** in **each** column, a **headline** sits **upper area** (typically upper-left alignment within the column), with a **subtitle** directly beneath. Both live in **negative space** above the device cluster.
- **Hierarchy:** headline is **largest and boldest**; subtitle is **smaller and lighter**, with consistent vertical rhythm between the two.
- **Margins:** generous **top** breathing room within the column before the headline; lateral margins keep type clear of column edges and gutters.
- **Wrapping:** headline may use **multi-line** wrap; subtitle usually **one or two lines** at this width.
- **Count:** **one** headline + **one** subtitle pair per column (no second competing headline stack in the same column).

## Device frames

- **Model read:** modern smartphone with **notch / island** silhouette; **dark** frame; believable bezel and glass.
- **Count per column (pattern):** columns **one and two** lean **dense** (**two** overlapping or staggered frames); columns **three through five** use **one dominant** frame each for clarity at a smaller beat width.
- **Pose:** mix of **tilted / isometric** pairs in the dense columns and **near-frontal** or slight tilt for single-phone columns; vary angle **column to column** so the strip does not feel templated.
- **Layering:** where two phones appear, they **overlap in depth** with explicit **z-order** (one hero, one support).
- **Screen content (role only, column order):**
  1. **Column 1:** top device suggests **media grid + long-form entry**; lower device suggests **active audio capture** (waveform / metering chrome).
  2. **Column 2:** one device suggests **rich text or note editor with system keyboard**; the other suggests **timed capture session** (elapsed time, primary record control).
  3. **Column 3:** **vertical timeline** or month-scoped **chronology** with **dated rows**, **inline thumbnails**, and a **bottom tab bar** with multiple destinations.
  4. **Column 4:** **note detail** with **horizontal media strip** above body copy; **row of compact action chips** for writing assistance; optional **central branded AI affordance** at the bottom of the screen chrome.
  5. **Column 5:** **grouped list** of entries with **leading thumbnails** and **secondary metadata** (e.g. region labels); one row shows **swipe-reveal** with **three** distinct **destructive / neutral / edit-class** actions on the exposed rail — describe as **interaction pattern**, not as fixed button labels.
- **Scale:** hero phone **largest** in its cluster; secondary phone **smaller** and partially occluded where two frames are used.

## Invariants

- **Text:** headline and subtitle must stay **fully legible**, **non-overlapping** with each other, **outside** device bezels and glare, and **never covered** by frames or shadows.
- **Devices:** may **tilt**, **stack**, **overlap**, and **crop** at column edges for continuity; **no** requirement that every bezel stays inside one column if the brief allows bleed for drama (gutters still read as column boundaries for type).

## Composition notes

- **Five beats** compress horizontal space per column versus a three-column strip: favor **one clear focal phone** whenever using a single device so UI remains readable at export scale.
- **Rhythm:** alternate **two-device density** with **single-device clarity** down the strip to avoid visual fatigue.
- Avoid placing headline blocks in the **lower half** of a column unless the plan proves **safe-zone** clearance above the device cluster.
